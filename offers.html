Fee-related information

What our prices cover.

"From" is the price for the scope shown on each offer page. Your written proposal confirms the final scope and price before you sign.

*A licensed real estate agent handles the home search. The agent's fee depends on the contract and the home you choose, and comes on top of this price. For some services, such as the home search, the agent or provider may pay us a commission out of the fee you pay them. We add nothing to that fee, and we confirm any commission in writing before you sign with them. Beyond the offer price, we charge nothing else for the search.

Family, Executive and Signature Arrival: 50% on signature, 30% on arrival, 20% at the end of the assignment.

Not covered by our offers: the fees of lawyers, tax advisors, notaries and other licensed professionals, sworn translations, government and visa fees, movers, schools, deposits and rent. See our terms and conditions.

Prices are in euros. The price including VAT is what you pay us for the scope in your written proposal. Anything else we charge, such as extra time, is agreed with you in writing first. Before you sign, your written proposal shows the final scope, the amount excluding VAT, the VAT and the total.
