Fee-related information

What you pay us, and what you pay directly to others.

  • *A licensed real estate agent handles the home search. The agent's fee, typically 1 to 2 months of rent, depends on the contract and the home you choose and comes on top of this price. You pay it directly to the agent. For some services, such as the home search, we receive a commission from the agent or partner, paid out of the fee you pay them. We charge you nothing on top for it, and we confirm it in writing before you sign. Beyond the offer price, we charge nothing else for the search.
  • Family, Executive and Signature Arrival: 50% on signature, 30% on arrival, 20% at the end of the assignment.
  • Paid directly by you: lawyers, tax advisors, notaries, sworn translations, government and visa fees, movers, schools, deposits and rent.
  • Prices are in euros. The price including VAT is what you pay us for the scope in your written proposal. Anything else we charge, such as extra time, is agreed with you in writing first. Amounts you pay directly to others, including the agent's fee, are not part of that price. The agent passes part of that fee to us, as we confirm in writing. Before you sign, your written proposal shows the final scope, the amount excluding VAT, the VAT and the total.

Stay Well

For families and individuals in Paris, French or international, who want regular concierge support from one contact.

  • €1,250 per month, for a family or an individual, including VAT (€1,041.67 excluding VAT), for 7 hours each month, including 3 in person, within the scope of our services.
  • Monthly price with a 3-month minimum commitment, billed monthly. After the minimum, it renews automatically each month unless you cancel at least 15 days before the next renewal date.
  • If you have never been a client, a one-time file fee of €180 including VAT (€150 excluding VAT) per named file is charged on your first Stay Well invoice, in addition to the monthly price. A couple shares one named file, and requests about your children fall under it at no additional file fee. It covers opening your file and a first conversation about your practical needs. It does not include legal, immigration or tax advice. The file fee is not refundable, subject to the rules that protect consumers, including any right of withdrawal.
  • We currently offer Stay Well in English. If you prefer another language, tell us before you commit, and we will confirm in writing what we can do.
  • Additional time is €225 per hour including VAT (€187.50 excluding VAT), only with your approval first. Unused hours do not carry over to the next month.
  • The contract is in your own name. If your employer reimburses you, that is between you and your employer, and we take no part in it.
  • Your written proposal confirms who is covered. Cancellation and withdrawal terms are set out in writing before you sign anything.
