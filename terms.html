Terms and conditions

Last updated: November 2026. These terms describe how Arrive Well Concierge, your relocation concierge in Paris, works with you. Your written proposal sets out the scope, the price and the terms that apply to your move or to your concierge support, and it prevails over this page.

Our role

We coordinate, prepare and follow up: your plan, your calendar, your documents and your appointments, for your relocation to Paris and your daily life here.

We do not provide legal, tax, immigration, financial or medical advice. That advice comes directly from the relevant licensed professional. Property search, negotiation, leases and sales are for a licensed real estate agent and, for a sale, a notary.

You sign your own documents and keep control of your official accounts.

Professionals and providers

We introduce you to licensed professionals and to providers, and you choose who to engage. With your agreement, we follow your file with them.

Their fees are not covered by the package or the monthly price you take with us. Lawyers, tax advisors, accountants, notaries, real estate agents, doctors, sworn translators, movers, schools, drivers, hotels, tradespeople and household staff are engaged by you and paid directly by you, as are government and visa fees, rent and deposits, unless your written proposal says otherwise.

For some services, such as the home search, the agent or provider may pay us a commission out of the fee you pay them. We add nothing to that fee, and we confirm any commission in writing before you sign with them. We never take two commissions on the same service, and we tell you when a company linked to us is involved.

Prices and payment

Prices are in euros and shown including VAT, then excluding VAT. Your written proposal shows the final scope, the amount excluding VAT, the VAT and the total before you sign.

  • Family Arrival, Executive Arrival and Signature Arrival: 50% on signature, 30% on arrival, 20% at the end of the assignment.
  • The Arrival Blueprint: payment terms are confirmed in writing before you sign.
  • Stay Well and Stay Well Signature: billed monthly, with a 3-month minimum. After the minimum, your concierge support renews each month unless you cancel at least 15 days before the next renewal date. A one-time file fee applies to new clients; it is not refundable, subject to the rules that protect consumers, including any right of withdrawal.
  • Corporate Arrival: quote and scope confirmed in writing before work starts, with payment in full at contract signature.
  • Additional time is billed only with your approval first.

Response times

We reply by the next working day. We aim to handle simple requests, such as booking a taxi or a doctor's appointment, the same day. We are not an emergency service. Your written proposal lists our working days.

In-person support takes place in Paris and Île-de-France, on weekdays during the day. Travel elsewhere is reviewed case by case, on written terms.

Timing of your move

Please contact us at least 2 months before you arrive in Paris. Arriving sooner? Let's talk. We tell you what we can take on, and any additional fee is quoted in writing before you decide.

What we do not promise

We do not promise a visa, a residence permit, a lease, a purchase, a school place or a bank account. Those decisions belong to the authorities, landlords, sellers, schools and banks. We promise the work.

When a decision goes against you, we tell you. Your professional advises you on the options, and we organize the next steps you choose.

Your documents and your data

Please do not send passports, residence permits or other sensitive documents by message. We tell you how to share them. Our privacy policy explains how we handle your information, and your written proposal sets out the data protection terms for your file.

Discretion is our rule. We never name you as a client, in public or to other clients, without your prior consent.

Cancellation and withdrawal

Your written proposal sets out your cancellation terms and, where it applies, your right of withdrawal and how to use it. Nothing on this page limits the rights that consumer protection rules give you.
