Questions from families

When should we start planning a family move to Paris?

Family Arrival runs from about 3 months before you arrive. We need your request at least 2 months before arrival. For a September school start, contact us well before that, because school applications often open months ahead.

Can you get our children into a school?

We search with you, prepare applications and organize visits and enrollment paperwork. The decision belongs to the school, so we do not promise a place.

Can we rent in Paris with American income?

Landlords decide case by case, so we cannot promise a lease. We prepare your rental file with you for French landlords, and a licensed real estate agent handles the search and the lease.

Will someone be there if we arrive in August?

During an assignment, we provide in-person and remote support every month of the year. For a late-August arrival, we aim to finish the paperwork we handle before August, so please contact us early.

What does Family Arrival cost?

From €22,500 including VAT (€18,750 excluding VAT) for up to 2 children, and €1,500 including VAT (€1,250 excluding VAT) for each additional child. On top: the real estate agent's fee, typically 1 to 2 months of rent, which depends on the contract and the home you choose; and other third-party costs such as movers, school fees and deposits. For some services, such as the home search, we receive a commission from the agent or partner, paid out of the fee you pay them. We charge you nothing on top for it, and we confirm it in writing before you sign.

Do you give tax or immigration advice?

No. A French-American tax advisor and an immigration lawyer advise you directly, and you pay them directly. We coordinate the logistics with them.
