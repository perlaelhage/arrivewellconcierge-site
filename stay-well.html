Who it's for

Families and individuals in Paris, French or international, who want regular concierge support from one contact who gets to know their household. You do not need to have moved with us.

We provide support in English and French, and in Arabic with Perla, co-founder. If you prefer another language, tell us before you commit, and we will confirm in writing what we can do.

What it brings

Paris is meant to be lived fully. With premium concierge support, we follow your French administrative paperwork and help organize your daily life: your everyday paperwork coordinated, and introductions to tradespeople and household help. For housekeepers and nannies, we hold first interviews and prepare a shortlist, counted in your monthly hours. You choose, and you remain the employer.

One contact gets to know your household, so each request starts from what we already know.

Want a co-founder as your contact, and a visit to your home every two weeks while you travel? See Stay Well Signature

What's included each month

  • 7 hours of support, including 3 in-person hours, within the scope of our services
  • A monthly check-in on open items and the upcoming dates on your file
  • Coordination of renewals and everyday paperwork. You sign your own documents, by electronic signature where the recipient accepts it. Legal questions go to a licensed lawyer.
  • Handling of the documents you choose to entrust to us, under the data protection terms in your written proposal
  • Introductions to tradespeople and household help, and help with reservations and appointments

Professionals and providers, arranged for you

  • Legal, immigration, tax and medical advice: licensed professionals. We can book the appointments and, with your agreement, follow your file with them, counted in your monthly hours.
  • Third-party costs, such as tradespeople, staff, tickets and travel, are not part of your monthly price (see our terms and conditions). Where a commission or referral fee applies to a service, we confirm it in writing before you sign with that provider.
  • Requests beyond our services, such as personal assistant work: we can suggest another provider.
  • We decline any request that is illegal or unethical.

How it works

  1. Start. When your Family Arrival or Executive Arrival ends or, if you are not moving with us, on the date in your signed proposal.
  2. Your requests. Send them by message. Please do not send passports, residence permits or other sensitive documents this way. We confirm the scope and the time needed before starting anything sizable.
  3. Each month. You get a short summary of hours used and items still open.

Price

€1,250 per month including VAT (€1,041.67 excluding VAT), with a 3-month minimum

For a family or an individual. Additional time is €225 per hour including VAT (€187.50 excluding VAT), only with your approval first. À la carte covers a single task at that same rate, with a 2-hour minimum.

Not covered by this price: tradespeople, household staff, tickets, travel and the fees of licensed professionals. See our terms and conditions.

New to us? A one-time file fee of €180 including VAT (€150 excluding VAT) is added to your first Stay Well invoice, for opening your file. One file per couple, children included. Former clients pay no new file fee.

Terms

  • Monthly price with a 3-month minimum commitment, billed monthly. Your written proposal confirms who is covered.
  • After the 3-month minimum, it renews automatically each month unless you cancel at least 15 days before the next renewal date.
  • If you move out of Paris for good during the minimum, including elsewhere in Île-de-France, you can cancel with 15 days' notice.
  • After the 3-month minimum, you can pause for up to 2 months per contract year, in up to 3 periods. We do not charge you for Stay Well during a pause, and there is no new file fee when you resume.
  • Stay Well is for families and individuals, with the contract in your own name.
  • The file fee covers opening your file and, once you have signed, a first conversation about your practical needs. It does not include legal, immigration or tax advice. The file fee is not refundable, subject to the rules that protect consumers, including any right of withdrawal. Your written proposal sets out these terms.
  • Unused hours do not carry over to the next month.
  • In-person hours take place on weekdays during the day. While your Stay Well is active, we provide in-person and remote support every month of the year.
  • Paris and Île-de-France. Travel elsewhere is reviewed case by case, on written terms.
  • Cancellation, withdrawal and renewal terms are confirmed in writing before you sign.

Our written commitment

  • One named contact who gets to know your household.
  • A reply by the next working day.
  • No extra hours billed without your approval.
  • A clear monthly summary of your hours.
  • A copy of every document we handle for you.
  • Total transparency on our fees and any commission, and regular written updates on your file.

We do not promise that every request can be met. When one cannot, we tell you and suggest an alternative where we can.
