The paperwork doesn't end when the move does. Neither do we.

Who it's for

Families and individuals living in Paris who want regular concierge support from people who get to know their household. You do not need to have moved with us.

The problem it solves

French life keeps sending paperwork, renewals and practical questions after the move is over. You have one contact who gets to know your file, so you do not have to start again with someone new each time.

The founders

One contact who gets to know you, with the team behind them. Meet the founders

What's included each month

  • 7 hours of support, including 4 in-person hours, within the scope of our services
  • A monthly check-in on open items and upcoming deadlines
  • Coordination of renewals and everyday paperwork, with documents you sign yourself; legal questions go to a licensed lawyer
  • Arranging tradespeople, household help, reservations and appointments

What's not included

  • Legal, tax and medical advice: licensed professionals, paid directly by you
  • Third-party costs: tradespeople, staff, tickets and travel, paid directly by you
  • Personal assistant work and requests outside our services: we can suggest another provider
  • Requests that are illegal or unethical: declined

How it works

  1. Starts when your Family Arrival or Executive Arrival ends or, if you already live in Paris, on the date in your signed proposal.
  2. Send requests by message. We confirm the scope and the time needed before starting anything sizable.
  3. Each month, you get a short summary of hours used and items still open.

Price and terms

Additional time is €180 per hour including VAT (€150 excluding VAT), only with your approval first.
