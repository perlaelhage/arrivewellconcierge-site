Stay Well

The paperwork doesn't end when the move does. Neither do we.

Book your free call

Who it's for

Families and individuals in Paris, French or international, who want regular concierge support from people who get to know their household. You do not need to have moved with us. We currently work with clients in English. If you prefer another language, tell us before you commit, and we will confirm in writing what we can do.

The problem it solves

French life keeps sending paperwork, renewals and practical questions after the move is over. You have one contact who gets to know your file, so you do not have to start again with someone new each time.

The founders

One contact who gets to know you, with the team behind them. Meet the founders

What's included each month

  • 7 hours of support, including 3 in-person hours, within the scope of our services
  • A monthly check-in on open items and the upcoming dates on your file
  • Coordination of renewals and everyday paperwork. You sign your own documents, by electronic signature through secure digital tools where the recipient accepts it. Legal questions go to a licensed lawyer.
  • Handling of the documents you choose to entrust to us, under the data protection terms in your written proposal
  • Introductions to tradespeople and household help, and help with reservations and appointments

What's not included

  • Legal, immigration, tax and medical advice: licensed professionals, paid directly by you. We can book the appointments.
  • Third-party costs: tradespeople, staff, tickets and travel, paid directly by you. Where a commission or referral fee applies to a service, we confirm it in writing before you sign with that provider.
  • Personal assistant work and requests outside our services: we can suggest another provider
  • Requests that are illegal or unethical: declined

How it works

  1. Starts when your Family Arrival or Executive Arrival ends or, if you are not moving with us, on the date in your signed proposal.
  2. Send requests by message. Please do not send passports, residence permits or other sensitive documents this way. We confirm the scope and the time needed before starting anything sizable.
  3. Each month, you get a short summary of hours used and items still open.
