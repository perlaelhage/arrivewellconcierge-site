Who it's for

Families and individuals in Paris, French or international, who want regular concierge support from people who get to know their household. You do not need to have moved with us. We currently offer Stay Well in English. If you prefer another language, tell us before you commit, and we will confirm in writing what we can do.

What it brings

Steady support for your household, month after month. One contact who knows your file and your home coordinates renewals, paperwork and practical questions as they come, within your monthly hours. Each request starts from what we already know.

What's included each month

  • 7 hours of support, including 3 in-person hours, within the scope of our services
  • A monthly check-in on open items and the upcoming dates on your file
  • Coordination of renewals and everyday paperwork. You sign your own documents, by electronic signature where the recipient accepts it. Legal questions go to a licensed lawyer.
  • Handling of the documents you choose to entrust to us, under the data protection terms in your written proposal
  • Introductions to tradespeople and household help, and help with reservations and appointments

Professionals and providers, arranged for you

  • Lawyers and tax advisors: we introduce you, book the appointments and, with your agreement, follow your file with them, counted in your monthly hours. Legal, immigration and tax advice is theirs, and you pay them directly.
  • Doctors: we book the appointments. Medical advice is theirs.
  • Tradespeople and household help: we make the introductions and set the appointments. Tickets and travel: we request reservations in your name. You pay each provider directly. Where a commission or referral fee applies to a service, we confirm it in writing before you sign with that provider.
  • Requests beyond our services, such as personal assistant work: we suggest another provider.
  • We decline any request that is illegal or unethical.

How it works

  1. Starts when your Family Arrival or Executive Arrival ends or, if you are not moving with us, on the date in your signed proposal.
  2. Send requests by message. Please do not send passports, residence permits or other sensitive documents this way. We confirm the scope and the time needed before starting anything sizable.
  3. Each month, you get a short summary of hours used and items still open.

Our service commitment

  • One named contact who gets to know your household.
  • A reply by the next working day.
  • No extra hours billed without your approval.
  • A clear monthly summary of your hours.
  • A copy of every document we handle for you.
  • If a step is blocked, we tell you what happened and what your options are.

We do not promise that every request can be met. When one cannot, we tell you and suggest an alternative where we can.
