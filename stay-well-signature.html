Stay Well Signature

Your home and paperwork in Paris, followed closely, even when you are away.

€1,750 per month incl. VAT, with a 3-month minimum

Book your discovery call

Who it's for

Households in Paris who want a cofounder as their contact and their home visited every two weeks while they travel. It suits those who have stepped back from work especially well: people who stay for years, travel often and want a social life in Paris.

Arabic-speaking households work with Perla, cofounder, in Arabic or English. Discretion is our rule: we will not name you as a client without your written agreement.

What it brings

A cofounder who gets to know your household, more hours each month, and your home visited every two weeks while you are away, with a short report and photos after each visit.

What's included each month

  • Everything in Stay Well
  • 9 hours of support instead of 7, including 4 in-person hours instead of 3
  • A cofounder as your dedicated contact
  • A reply the same day to requests received before noon on one of our working days, as listed in your proposal; otherwise by the next working day
  • Home watch while you are away: a visit every two weeks, mail, plants, heating and tradespeople let in, on the key and access terms in your proposal, with a short report and photos. Visits count toward your hours.
  • Your return prepared: home aired, basic groceries, and your first week's appointments requested in advance
  • A companion at appointments in English (bank, administration, doctor), counted in your hours. We accompany you; we do not act as a medical interpreter.
  • A yearly household calendar and a quarterly check-in at your home: renewals, insurance, school dates and documents to gather for your tax advisor
  • Household staff followed up: schedules, holiday cover and help finding a replacement, led by Christelle. You remain the employer.
  • Reservations requested for you as early as possible, and a monthly cultural selection: shows, exhibitions, restaurants. You pay for your tickets.
  • Visiting family and friends: hotel, transfers and outings arranged in their name; providers are paid directly

For a life beyond work

  • Travel with your home visited and your return prepared
  • Your residence-permit renewals: calendar, documents from your lawyer's list, and appointments coordinated with your lawyer. Advice stays with your lawyer.
  • Healthcare in English: we introduce you to English-speaking doctors and specialists and book your appointments. We give no medical advice.
  • French online services: we show you how to use the health insurance, tax, medical booking and online banking sites, and you enter your own login details. What to declare stays with your tax advisor.
  • A social life: cultural outings, French lessons, and introductions to English-speaking associations
  • Grandchildren's visits arranged
  • Small works and home adjustments: quoted and invoiced by the professionals you choose. Any coordination by us is agreed separately, in writing.

Professionals and providers, arranged for you

  • Legal, immigration, tax and medical advice: licensed professionals, paid directly by you. We can book the appointments and, with your agreement, follow your file with them, counted in your monthly hours.
  • Third-party costs: tradespeople, staff, tickets and travel, paid directly by you. Where a commission or referral fee applies to a service, we confirm it in writing before you sign with that provider.
  • Requests beyond our services, such as personal assistant work: we can suggest another provider.
  • We decline any request that is illegal or unethical.

How it works

  1. Start. On the date in your signed proposal, with a cofounder as your contact.
  2. Your requests. Send them by message. Please do not send passports, residence permits or other sensitive documents this way. We confirm the scope and the time needed before starting anything sizable.
  3. Each month. You get a summary of your hours, and a report after each home visit.

Price

€1,750 per month including VAT (€1,458.33 excluding VAT), with a 3-month minimum

For a family or an individual. Additional time is €225 per hour including VAT (€187.50 excluding VAT), only with your approval first.

If you have never been a client, the one-time file fee of €180 including VAT (€150 excluding VAT) applies, on your first invoice.

Paid directly by you: licensed professionals, tradespeople, staff, tickets and travel.

Terms

  • As for Stay Well, including the 3-month minimum, monthly renewal, pause, cancellation and withdrawal terms.
  • We take on a limited number of Stay Well Signature households, as each has a cofounder as its contact.
  • Cancellation, withdrawal and renewal terms are confirmed in writing before you sign.

Our written commitment

  • A cofounder who knows your household.
  • No extra hours billed without your approval.
  • A clear monthly summary of your hours and a report after each home visit.
  • If a step is blocked, we tell you what happened and what your options are.

We do not promise that every request can be met. When one cannot, we tell you and suggest an alternative where we can.
