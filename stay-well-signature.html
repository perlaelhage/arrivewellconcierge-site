Stay Well Signature

Your home and paperwork in Paris, followed closely, even when you are away.

Speak with a cofounder

Who it's for

Households in Paris who want a cofounder as their contact and their home checked every two weeks while they travel. It suits retirees especially well: people who stay for years, travel often and want a social life in Paris.

How it differs from Stay Well

Everything in Stay Well, plus three things Stay Well does not have: a cofounder as your dedicated contact, a reply the same day to requests received before noon on our working days, and a visit to your home every two weeks while you are away. You also get 9 hours of support each month instead of 7, including 4 in person instead of 3. Home visits count toward those hours.

The founders

We take on a limited number of Stay Well Signature households, as each has a cofounder as its contact. Meet the founders

What's included each month

  • Everything in Stay Well
  • 9 hours of support, including 4 in-person hours, within the scope of our services
  • A cofounder as your dedicated contact
  • A reply the same day to requests received before noon on one of our working days, as listed in your proposal; otherwise by the next working day
  • Home visits while you are away: a visit every two weeks to collect mail, water plants, check the heating and let tradespeople in, on the key and access terms in your proposal, with a short report and photos. Visits count toward your hours. This is not a security or guarding service.
  • Your return prepared: home aired, basic groceries, and your first week's appointments requested in advance
  • A companion at appointments in English (bank, administration, doctor), counted in your hours. We accompany you; we do not act as a medical interpreter.
  • A yearly household calendar and a quarterly check-in at your home: renewals, insurance, school dates, and documents to gather for your tax advisor
  • Household staff followed up: schedules, holiday cover, and help finding a replacement. You remain the employer.
  • Reservations requested for you as early as possible, and a monthly cultural selection: shows, exhibitions, restaurants. You pay for your tickets.
  • Visiting family and friends: hotel, transfers and outings arranged in their name; providers are paid directly

For retirees, in particular

  • Travel with your home checked and your return prepared
  • Your residence-permit renewals: calendar, documents from your lawyer's list, and appointments coordinated with your lawyer. Advice stays with your lawyer.
  • Healthcare in English: English-speaking doctors and specialists found, and appointments booked. We give no medical advice.
  • French online services: we show you how to use the health insurance, tax, medical booking and online banking sites, and you enter your own login details. What to declare stays with your tax advisor.
  • A social life: cultural outings, French lessons, and introductions to English-speaking associations and clubs
  • Grandchildren's visits arranged
  • Small works and home adjustments coordinated, quoted separately

Professionals and providers, arranged for you

  • Lawyers and tax advisors: we introduce you, book the appointments and, with your agreement, follow your file with them, counted in your monthly hours. Legal, immigration and tax advice is theirs, and you pay them directly.
  • Doctors: we book the appointments. Medical advice is theirs.
  • Tradespeople and household help: we make the introductions and set the appointments. Tickets and travel: we request reservations in your name. You pay each provider directly. Where a commission or referral fee applies to a service, we confirm it in writing before you sign with that provider.
  • Requests beyond our services, such as personal assistant work: we suggest another provider.
  • We decline any request that is illegal or unethical.
