Signature Arrival

Both cofounders on your move, from the first scouting day to your one-year review.

Who it's for

Families, executives and individuals who want their home search, school applications and paperwork prepared well before they land, with both cofounders on their file. We take on a limited number of Signature Arrival households each year. It starts with a private conversation with a cofounder. You can also write to us first, on WhatsApp or by email.

If an employer pays any part of the move, Corporate Arrival applies.

What it brings

A move at this level involves dozens of decisions, providers and appointments across two countries. You have one person to call, and both cofounders at work on your file before you arrive.

One cofounder is your named lead. Perla oversees children, schools and the home; Christelle oversees the paperwork. For Arabic-speaking households, Perla is the named lead, in Arabic or English. Administrative paperwork is handled in English or French, as each recipient requires.

Discretion is our rule: we will not name you as a client without your written agreement.

What's included

  • The Arrival Blueprint, included
  • Two scouting days in Paris before your move, organized and accompanied: neighborhoods, school visits and home viewings with a licensed agent. We help you arrange your hotel and transport, booked in your name and paid directly by you.
  • Schools: shortlist, accompanied visits, application files, preparation for children's admission interviews with a teacher we introduce you to, and enrollment paperwork; sign-up for activities; French lessons arranged for children and parents. Providers are paid directly by you. Schools make their own admission decisions.
  • Furnished temporary housing: options presented, and your booking arranged in your name once you choose. Any commission is confirmed in writing before you book.
  • Your permanent home: rental file, viewings with a licensed agent, and a detailed practical check of the home by Perla before you sign, which is not a technical inspection
  • Renting or buying: we support either. For a purchase, a licensed real estate agent handles the search and the negotiation, and your notary handles the sale. We coordinate your calendar, documents and appointments.
  • Your home prepared for your arrival, as far as your lease dates allow: utilities and internet setup coordinated, home insurance quotes from a broker, cleaning, your delivery received on the terms in your proposal, unpacking supervised, and first groceries
  • Arrival: a driver booked at the airport (paid by you), and a cofounder to welcome you at your home on a weekday, at a time agreed in your proposal
  • Paperwork: residence-permit logistics coordinated with your lawyer, with a cofounder at your appointments; introductions to banks, including private banking, which make their own decisions; phones; social security; health insurance through a broker; driver's license exchange formalities, where the authorities accept your license; car rental or purchase; pet arrival logistics with your vet
  • Household staff: help finding and interviewing a nanny or housekeeper, led by Christelle, and help with your employer paperwork. You remain the employer and pay staff directly.
  • Health: introductions to English-speaking doctors and specialists, first appointments booked, and a list of emergency numbers and contacts. We give no medical advice.
  • Life in Paris: a neighborhood address book, introductions to two or three English-speaking associations, and a cultural calendar for your first three months
  • Up to 16 in-person appointments
  • A reply the same day to requests received before noon on one of our working days, as listed in your proposal; otherwise by the next working day
  • A written update every week until you arrive
  • 4 months of follow-up after arrival, then 2 months of Stay Well Signature included. Your written proposal states what happens when those 2 months end.
  • A digital family file, kept under the data protection terms in your written proposal
  • An in-person review one year after your arrival

Your professionals, coordinated by us

Not part of your package price. Each professional advises you and bills you directly. With your agreement, we gather documents, book appointments and track deadlines.

  • Immigration, tax and legal advice: licensed professionals, paid directly by you
  • The agent's fee for the home search: paid directly to the agent. Before you sign with the agent, we confirm the fee in writing, along with any share the agent would pass to us.
  • Rent, deposits, school fees, government fees, movers, drivers, hotels, staff and other providers: paid directly by you
  • Interior designers: if you wish, we introduce you to several. Before any introduction, we tell you in writing when a designer is linked to our company or to one of our cofounders, and the choice is yours. Design and renovation are quoted and invoiced by the professionals you choose. Any coordination by us is agreed separately, in writing.

How it works

  1. A private conversation. With a cofounder: your household, your dates and what you expect from us.
  2. Your plan and scouting days. The Arrival Blueprint, then two accompanied days in Paris before your move.
  3. Before and around arrival. Schools, home and paperwork, with a written update every week until you arrive.
  4. After arrival. 4 months of follow-up, then 2 months of Stay Well Signature included, and an in-person review one year after your arrival.

Price

€36,000 including VAT (€30,000 excluding VAT)*

For one household, children included. The Arrival Blueprint is included. If you have already bought it on its own, half of its price, €1,125 including VAT (€937.50 excluding VAT), is deducted when you sign within 30 days of receiving your plan. Your written proposal confirms the final scope before you sign.

*The real estate agent's fee is not in this price. It depends on the contract and the home you choose, and is paid directly to the agent. Any share the agent would pass to us comes out of the agent's fee, and we add nothing to it. Before you sign with the agent, we confirm in writing the fee the agent quotes and the amount of any such share. Beyond your package, we bill nothing else for the home search.

Paid directly by you: licensed professionals, rent, deposits, school fees, government fees, movers, drivers, hotels, staff and other providers.

Terms

  • 50% on signature, 30% on arrival, 20% at the end of the assignment.
  • Requests must reach us at least 2 months before arrival; for a school-year start, earlier is better, as schools decide early.
  • In-person appointments take place on weekdays during the day. During your assignment, we provide in-person and remote support every month of the year.
  • Paris and Île-de-France. Travel elsewhere is reviewed case by case, on written terms.
  • Any commission or referral fee from a provider is confirmed in writing before you sign with them.
  • Cancellation and withdrawal terms are confirmed in writing before you sign.

Our written commitment

  • Both cofounders on your file, with one named lead.
  • A written record of who is doing what, by when.
  • A copy of every document and letter we handle for you.
  • If a step is blocked, we tell you what happened and what your options are.

We do not promise a visa, a lease, a school place or a bank account. Those decisions belong to landlords, schools, banks and the authorities. We promise the work.
