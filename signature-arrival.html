Signature Arrival

Both cofounders on your move, from the first scouting day to your one-year review.

Speak with a cofounder

We take on a limited number of Signature Arrival households each year. It starts with a private conversation.

Who it's for

Families, executives and individuals who want their home search, school applications and paperwork prepared well before they land, with both cofounders on their file. If an employer pays any part of the move, Corporate Arrival applies.

What it brings

A move prepared before you land, by two cofounders who attend to the details. They prepare each decision for you and coordinate providers and appointments between your current home and Paris. You have one person to call, so your first days in Paris can belong to your household, not to logistics.

Who you work with

Both cofounders. One is your named lead. Discretion is our rule: we never name a client without their agreement. Meet the founders

Before you move

  • The Arrival Blueprint, included
  • Two scouting days in Paris before your move, organized and accompanied: neighborhoods, school visits and home viewings with a licensed agent. We help you arrange your hotel and transport, booked in your name and paid directly by you.
  • Schools: shortlist, accompanied visits, application files and enrollment paperwork; sessions to prepare your children for admission interviews, arranged with a teacher you choose and pay directly, as confirmed in your written proposal; sign-up for activities; French lessons arranged for children and parents. Teachers and other providers are paid directly by you and are not part of our price. Schools make their own admission decisions.
  • Furnished temporary housing: we coordinate with the furnished residence or licensed agent you choose; you book in your own name and pay directly. Any commission is disclosed in writing before you book.
  • Your permanent home: your rental file prepared with you, viewings with a licensed agent, and a practical walk-through of the home by Perla before you sign. It is not a technical or legal inspection.
  • A written update every week until you arrive

Your arrival

  • Your home prepared for your arrival, as far as your lease dates allow: utilities and internet setup coordinated, home insurance quotes from a broker, cleaning, your delivery received on the terms in your proposal, unpacking supervised, and first groceries
  • A driver booked at the airport (paid by you), and a cofounder to welcome you at your home on a weekday, at a time agreed in your proposal
  • Paperwork: residence-permit logistics coordinated with your lawyer, with a cofounder at your appointments; introductions to banks, including private banking, which make their own decisions; phones; social security; health insurance through a broker; driver's license exchange formalities, where the authorities accept your license; car rental or purchase; pet arrival logistics with your vet
  • Household staff: help finding and interviewing a nanny or housekeeper, and help with your employer paperwork. You remain the employer and pay staff directly.
  • Health: introductions to English-speaking doctors and specialists, first appointments booked, and a list of emergency numbers and contacts. We give no medical advice.

Your first year

  • Life in Paris: a neighborhood address book, introductions to two or three clubs or associations, and a cultural calendar for your first three months
  • Up to 16 in-person appointments
  • A reply the same day to requests received before noon on one of our working days, as listed in your proposal; otherwise by the next working day
  • 4 months of follow-up after arrival, then 2 months of Stay Well Signature included. Your written proposal states what happens when those 2 months end.
  • A digital family file, kept under the data protection terms in your written proposal
  • An in-person review one year after your arrival

Your professionals, coordinated by us

We introduce you to licensed professionals in each field, and you choose who to engage. They advise you directly and invoice you directly. Then, with your agreement, we handle the administrative side with them for the length of your assignment, as set out in your written proposal: the documents they request gathered, appointments booked, the dates they set kept in view.

  • Immigration lawyers, tax advisors and other licensed professionals, for immigration, tax and legal advice
  • A licensed real estate agent, for the home search. You pay the agent's fee directly to the agent. Before you sign with the agent, we confirm the fee in writing, along with any share we receive.
  • Rent, deposits, school fees, government fees, movers, drivers, hotels, staff and other providers are paid directly by you.
  • Interior designers: if you wish, we introduce you to several. Before any introduction, we tell you in writing when a designer is linked to our company or to one of our cofounders, and the choice is yours. Design is quoted and invoiced separately by the designer you choose.
  • Renovation works: invoiced by the contractor you choose. If you ask us to coordinate works, we do so under a separate written agreement that states our fee before you sign.

How it works

  1. A private conversation with a cofounder about your household, your dates and what matters most to you.
  2. A written proposal that confirms the scope, the dates and the price.
  3. Your Arrival Blueprint, then two scouting days in Paris.
  4. Your arrival, with a written update every week until you land.
  5. 4 months of follow-up, 2 months of Stay Well Signature, and a review one year after your arrival.

Price and terms

€36,000 including VAT (€30,000 excluding VAT)

For one household, children included. The Arrival Blueprint is included. If you have already bought it on its own, half of its price, €1,125 including VAT (€937.50 excluding VAT), is deducted when you sign within 30 days of receiving your plan. The final scope is confirmed in a written proposal.

  • Requests must reach us at least 2 months before arrival; for a school-year start, earlier is better, as schools decide early.
  • In-person appointments take place on weekdays during the day. During your assignment, we provide in-person and remote support every month of the year.

Our service commitment

  • Both cofounders on your file, with one named lead.
  • A written record of who is doing what, by when.
  • A copy of every document and letter we handle for you.
  • If a step is blocked, we tell you what happened and what your options are.

We do not promise a visa, a lease, a school place or a bank account. Those decisions belong to landlords, schools, banks and the authorities. We promise the work.
