What we do, and what stays with licensed professionals

We coordinate, prepare and follow up: your plan, your calendar, your documents and your appointments. You sign your own documents and keep control of your accounts.

Legal, immigration, tax, financial and medical advice is provided directly by the relevant licensed professionals. Where we can, we introduce you to more than one professional, and the choice is always yours. With your agreement, we follow your files with them: documents gathered, appointments booked, dates kept in view, and regular follow-ups. Our terms and conditions explain how professionals' fees work.

For some services, such as the home search, the agent or provider may pay us a commission out of the fee you pay them. We add nothing to that fee, and we confirm any commission in writing before you sign with them. We never take two commissions on the same service, and we tell you when a company linked to us is involved.
