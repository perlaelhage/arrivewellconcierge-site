Together

We bring careful coordination and a working knowledge of French administration. From your first steps in Paris to your everyday needs, we work with precision and discretion. We never name a client without their agreement.

Paris should be experienced as a privilege, not a challenge. At Arrive Well, we work so that you don't just arrive: you arrive well.

What we do, and what we leave to licensed professionals

We coordinate, prepare and follow up: your plan, your calendar, your documents and your appointments. You sign your own documents and keep control of your accounts.

Legal, immigration, tax, financial and medical advice is provided directly by the relevant licensed professional, selected and paid by you. For some services, such as the home search, we receive a commission from the agent or partner, paid out of the fee you pay them. We charge you nothing on top for it, and we confirm it in writing before you sign. We never take two commissions on the same service, and we tell you when a company linked to us is involved.
