The Arrival Blueprint

Three calls. One written plan. Yours to keep, even if you go no further.

€2,250 incl. VAT

Book your discovery call

Who it's for

Individuals, couples and families considering a move to Paris who want a clear plan before committing to anything larger.

What it brings

Visa, housing, schools, banking and health coverage run on different timelines, and the order matters. You leave with the sequence, the deadlines and a realistic budget.

What's included

  • Three video calls with a co-founder
  • A written plan and timeline for your move
  • A comparison of three neighborhoods suited to your household
  • A shortlist of five schools with application dates, for families
  • A moving budget, including third-party costs to expect
  • A list of the licensed professionals you are likely to need, described by specialty

Beyond the plan

Licensed professionals advise you directly; their fees are not part of the Blueprint.

  • Legal and immigration advice: an immigration lawyer
  • Tax advice: a French-American tax advisor
  • Home search: a licensed real estate agent
  • Applications, bookings and in-person support: available through Family Arrival, Executive Arrival or Signature Arrival

How it works

  1. Discovery call. 15 minutes with a co-founder to check fit and answer practical questions. We tell you the price up front.
  2. First call. Your household, your dates, your priorities.
  3. Second call. Neighborhoods, schools, budget.
  4. Third call. We walk you through the written plan.

Allow about two weeks from your first call to your written plan.

Price

€2,250 including VAT (€1,875 excluding VAT)

If you sign a written proposal for Family Arrival, Executive Arrival or Signature Arrival within 30 days of receiving your plan, we deduct half of the Blueprint price, €1,125 including VAT (€937.50 excluding VAT), from that package price. Your proposal shows the deduction.

Not covered: the fees of any licensed professional you choose to consult. See our terms and conditions.

Terms

  • Payment terms are confirmed in writing before you sign.
  • Cancellation and withdrawal terms are confirmed in writing before you sign.

Our written commitment

  • The plan is yours to keep and use, with us or without us.
  • We reply by the next working day.
  • We tell you when your situation calls for a licensed professional.

We do not promise a visa, a lease, a school place or a bank account. Those decisions belong to the relevant authority or institution.
