The Arrival Blueprint

Three calls. One written plan. Yours to keep, even if you go no further.

Book your discovery call

Who it's for

Individuals and families considering a move to Paris who want a clear plan before committing to anything larger.

What it brings

Clarity before you commit. Visa, housing, schools, banking and health coverage each follow their own timeline; we set them in one order for you. You leave with the steps, the dates to watch and a budget estimate, yours to keep.

The founders

One of our two founders is your contact. Perla has 10 years of sales and team leadership at multinational companies, and real estate and interior design experience through her own companies. Christelle has 15 years of experience at prestigious American companies. Meet them

What's included

  • Three video calls with one contact
  • A written plan and timeline for your move
  • A comparison of three neighborhoods suited to your household
  • A shortlist of five schools with application dates, for families
  • A moving budget, including third-party costs to expect
  • A list of the licensed professionals you are likely to need, described by specialty

Beyond the plan

The Blueprint is the plan, not the work that follows. It sets out the professionals you are likely to need, and at what stage. They advise you directly, and you pay each of them directly. What you do next, with us or without us, is your choice.

  • An immigration lawyer, for legal and immigration advice
  • A French-American tax advisor, for tax advice
  • A licensed real estate agent, for the home search. Their fee is separate. For some services, such as the home search, we receive a commission from the agent or partner, paid out of the fee you pay them. We charge you nothing on top for it, and we confirm it in writing before you sign.
  • Applications, bookings, in-person support and the follow-up of your file with each professional begin with Family Arrival, Executive Arrival or Signature Arrival, each under its own proposal and price.

How it works

  1. A free 15-minute discovery call to check fit and answer practical questions. We tell you the price up front.
  2. First call: your household, your dates, your priorities.
  3. Second call: neighborhoods, schools, budget.
  4. Third call: we walk you through the written plan.

Allow about two weeks from your first call to your written plan.
