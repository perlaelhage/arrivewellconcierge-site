Executive Arrival

One contact to deal with, and a team coordinating behind the scenes.

Who it's for

Executives, entrepreneurs and individuals moving to Paris, paying for the move themselves. If your company pays or reimburses any part of your move, Corporate Arrival applies. Book the call yourself: we prepare a written quote you can pass on to your HR team.

What it brings

Our work is to keep your first weeks in Paris yours, for the new role or the new chapter. We organize permit appointments around the steps your immigration lawyer sets, alongside housing, banking and health coverage, and call on you where your signature or your presence is required.

The founders

Christelle has 15 years of experience at prestigious American companies. Perla has 10 years of sales and team leadership at multinational companies, and real estate and interior design experience through her own companies. She is also an interior designer.

What's included

  • A written plan and timeline, kept up to date
  • Coordination of residence-permit logistics: appointments, calendar and documents from the list your immigration lawyer provides
  • Coordination of temporary housing that you book and pay for directly
  • Housing coordination: an introduction to a licensed real estate agent, who handles the search, viewings, negotiation and lease; a rental file prepared with you for French landlords; and your calendar organized around the viewings your agent sets
  • Bank, phone, utilities, social security and health insurance setup support
  • English-speaking doctor and dentist introductions
  • Up to 4 in-person appointments
  • 1 month of follow-up after arrival

Your professionals, coordinated by us

We introduce you to licensed professionals in each field, and you choose who to engage. They advise you directly and invoice you directly. Then, with your agreement, we handle the administrative side with them for the length of your assignment, as set out in your written proposal: the documents they request gathered, appointments booked, the dates they set kept in view.

  • An immigration lawyer, for immigration advice and filings
  • A French-American tax advisor or accountant, for tax advice and filings
  • A licensed real estate agent, for the property search, negotiation and lease. Their fee, typically 1 to 2 months of rent, depends on the contract and the home you choose and is paid directly by you. For some services, such as the home search, we receive a commission from the agent or partner, paid out of the fee you pay them. We charge you nothing on top for it, and we confirm it in writing before you sign.
  • A lawyer and an accountant, for company formation and business-plan advice
  • Doctors and hospitals, for medical care. We make the introductions; your health stays between you and your doctor.
  • International movers, sworn translators and notaries
  • Interior designers: if you wish, we introduce you to several. Before any introduction, we tell you in writing when a designer is linked to our company or to one of our cofounders, and the choice is yours. Each designer quotes and invoices you directly.
  • Rent, deposits, government and visa fees, and movers are also paid directly by you.
  • Ongoing support after the first month: through Stay Well

How it works

  1. From about 2 months before arrival: your plan, documents and rental file.
  2. Around arrival: move-in, bank and health coverage, as each step allows.
  3. For 1 month after arrival: follow-up on open items, and we close the file with you.
