Executive Arrival

One contact to deal with, and a team coordinating behind the scenes.

Book your free call

Who it's for

Executives, entrepreneurs and couples without children moving to Paris, paying for the move themselves. If a company pays for your move, Corporate Arrival applies.

The problem it solves

You start a demanding role while permit appointments, housing, banking and health coverage all ask for your time. We organize the sequence and keep your involvement to what only you can do.

The founders

Christelle has 15 years of experience at prestigious American companies. Perla has 10 years of sales and team leadership at multinational companies, and real estate and interior design experience through her own companies. She is also an interior designer.

What's included

  • A written plan and timeline, kept up to date
  • Coordination of residence-permit logistics: appointments, calendar and documents from the list your immigration lawyer provides
  • Coordination of temporary housing that you book and pay for directly
  • Housing coordination: an introduction to a licensed real estate agent, who handles the search, viewings, negotiation and lease; a rental file prepared with you for French landlords; and your calendar organized around the viewings your agent sets
  • Bank, phone, utilities, social security and health insurance setup support
  • English-speaking doctor and dentist introductions
  • Up to 4 in-person appointments
  • 1 month of follow-up after arrival

What's not included

  • Immigration advice and filings: an immigration lawyer, paid directly by you
  • Tax advice and filings: a French-American tax advisor or accountant
  • Property search, negotiation and the lease: a licensed real estate agent. Their fee, typically about one month of rent, depends on the home you choose and is paid directly by you. For some services, such as the home search, we receive a commission from the agent or partner, paid out of the fee you pay them. We charge you nothing on top for it, and we confirm it in writing before you sign.
  • Company formation and business-plan advice: a lawyer and an accountant
  • Medical care: doctors and hospitals
  • International movers, sworn translators and notaries: paid directly
  • Ongoing support after the first month: available through Stay Well

How it works

  1. From about 2 months before arrival: your plan, documents and rental file.
  2. Around arrival: move-in, bank and health coverage, as each step allows.
  3. For 1 month after arrival: follow-up on open items, and we close the file with you.
