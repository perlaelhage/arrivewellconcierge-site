Executive Arrival

One contact to deal with, and a team coordinating behind the scenes.

From €12,000 incl. VAT*

Book your discovery call

Who it's for

Executives, entrepreneurs and individuals moving to Paris, paying for the move themselves. If your company pays or reimburses any part of it, Corporate Arrival applies.

What it brings

Permit appointments, housing, banking and health coverage all ask for your time at once. We organize the sequence and keep your involvement to what only you can do.

Meet the founders

What's included

  • A written plan and timeline, kept up to date
  • Coordination of residence-permit logistics: appointments, calendar and documents from the list your lawyer provides
  • Temporary housing support
  • Home search: we introduce you to a licensed real estate agent, who leads the search, and we stay by your side throughout. A rental file prepared for French landlords, and first viewings led by the agent, which we can attend for you and report on in writing, so you visit only the homes you select
  • Renting or buying: we support either. For a purchase, a licensed real estate agent handles the search and the negotiation, and your notary handles the sale. We coordinate your calendar, documents and appointments.
  • Bank, phone, utilities, social security and health insurance setup support
  • English-speaking doctor and dentist introductions
  • Up to 4 in-person appointments
  • 1 month of follow-up and support after your arrival

Your professionals, coordinated by us

Licensed professionals advise you directly; their fees are not part of your package (see our terms and conditions). With your agreement, we gather documents, book appointments and track deadlines.

  • Immigration advice and complex filings: an immigration lawyer
  • Tax advice and filings: a French-American tax advisor or accountant
  • Property search, negotiation and the lease or sale: a licensed real estate agent, with your notary for a sale. The agent's fee depends on the contract and the home you choose, and is set out in the agent's mandate. If the agent would pass part of it to us, we confirm it in writing before you sign with the agent.
  • Company formation and business-plan advice: a lawyer and an accountant
  • Medical care: doctors and hospitals
  • International movers, sworn translators, notaries
  • Ongoing support after the first month: available through Stay Well or, with home visits while you travel, Stay Well Signature

How it works

  1. Before arrival. From about 2 months out: your plan, documents and rental file.
  2. Around arrival. Your home, bank and health coverage.
  3. After arrival. For 1 month: follow-up on open items, then we close the file with you.

Price

From €12,000 including VAT (€10,000 excluding VAT)*

"From" is the price for the services listed on this page. Your written proposal confirms the final scope, the VAT and the total before you sign.

*The real estate agent's fee is not in this price. It depends on the contract and the home you choose. Any share the agent would pass to us comes out of the agent's fee, and we add nothing to it. Before you sign with the agent, we confirm in writing the fee the agent quotes and the amount of any share the agent would pass to us. Beyond your package, we bill nothing else for the home search. If a landlord asks for a guarantor, we can help you prepare an application to a professional guarantor company of your choice. The company decides whether to accept it, and its fee is separate.

Not covered by this price: the fees of licensed professionals such as an immigration lawyer, government and visa fees, movers, deposits and rent. See our terms and conditions. If a provider would pay us a commission or referral fee, we confirm it in writing before you sign with them.

If your company pays or reimburses any part of the move, Corporate Arrival applies instead: everything in Executive Arrival, plus a weekly update to your HR team, on a quote we send your company. Tell us on your discovery call, and we prepare a Corporate Arrival quote for your company.

Terms

  • 50% on signature, 30% on arrival, 20% at the end of the assignment.
  • Requests must reach us at least 2 months before arrival. Arriving sooner? Let's talk.
  • In-person appointments take place on weekdays during the day; weekends are not included. Evening calls can be scheduled on request. During your assignment, we provide in-person and remote support every month of the year.
  • Paris and Île-de-France. Travel elsewhere is reviewed case by case, on written terms.
  • Third-party costs are not part of your package.
  • You sign your own documents and keep control of your official accounts.
  • Cancellation and withdrawal terms are confirmed in writing before you sign.

Our written commitment

  • One named contact from day one, with a team coordinating behind the scenes.
  • A written record of who is doing what, by when.
  • A copy of every document and letter we handle for you.
  • A reply by the next working day.
  • Total transparency on our fees and any commission, and regular written updates on your file.

We do not promise a visa, a lease or a bank account. Those decisions belong to landlords, banks and the authorities. We promise the work.
