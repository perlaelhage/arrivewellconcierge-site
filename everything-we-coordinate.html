Everything we can coordinate

From your first plan to daily life in Paris. This is the range we draw from: each offer includes a defined part of it, and other services can be added à la carte.

Book your discovery call

Your written proposal sets out the services chosen for you, and their terms. Professionals and providers are chosen by you; their fees are separate (see our terms and conditions). For some services, such as the home search, the agent or provider may pay us a commission out of the fee you pay them. We add nothing to that fee, and we confirm any commission in writing before you sign with them. See fee-related information

Before you arrive

Relocation plan and timeline; coordination of visa and residence-permit logistics with your immigration lawyer; introduction to a French-American tax advisor; neighborhood guidance and tours; school pre-selection and application calendar; moving budget and checklist.

Your home

Temporary housing coordination; an introduction to a licensed real estate agent, who handles the search, viewings and negotiation, whether you rent or buy; for a purchase, your notary handles the sale; your rental file prepared with you; your calendar organized around the viewings your agent sets; utilities, internet and home insurance setup support; coordination with your movers, who handle customs formalities. Works and design are quoted and invoiced by the professionals you choose. Any coordination by us is agreed separately, in writing.

Arrival and paperwork

Airport welcome and first-day orientation; bank account opening support; French mobile line; residence-permit logistics and appointments; social security registration; complementary health insurance setup support; driver's license exchange formalities, where the authorities accept your license; car purchase and registration; informal, non-sworn translation of everyday letters; letters with legal, tax or immigration content go to the relevant licensed professional.

Legal, tax and financial, with licensed professionals

Introductions to immigration lawyers; notaries; accountants and tax advisors; banks, including private banking, and wealth managers, who make their own decisions; insurance brokers; sworn translators. These professionals advise you directly. We receive no commission from lawyers, notaries, doctors, accountants, tax advisors, schools, banks, insurers or brokers. For any other service, if a provider would pay us a commission or referral fee, we confirm it in writing before you sign with that provider.

Children and education

Search for international, bilingual, private or public French schools; applications, visits and interviews; public school enrollment at the town hall; daycare and nursery applications; introductions to English-speaking nannies and au pairs; employer paperwork support, quoted separately; French lessons, tutoring and activities; holiday camps.

Health and well-being

Introductions to English-speaking family doctors, pediatricians, dentists and specialists; emergency numbers to keep at hand; gyms, yoga, spas and wellness addresses.

Settling in as a family

Introductions to English-speaking associations and communities; support for your partner: networking, CV for the French job market, social life.

Daily life and concierge

Home help and housekeeping; nannies and babysitters; groceries; private chef and catering; car rental, drivers and airport transfers; flight, hotel, restaurant and event bookings; tradespeople; pet travel, veterinarian, dog walking and pet sitting; gifts and special occasions; French lessons for adults.

When you leave Paris

End of lease and move-out inspection; closing contracts and accounts; departure move.

Need something else in Paris? Just ask. We confirm in writing what we can take on, and on what terms. Requests that are illegal or unethical are declined.
