Everything we can coordinate

From your first plan to daily life in Paris. This is the range we draw from: each offer includes a defined part of it, and other services can be added à la carte.

Book your discovery call

Your written proposal sets out the services chosen for you, and their terms. Professionals and providers are chosen and paid by you directly. For some services, such as the home search, we receive a commission from the agent or partner, paid out of the fee you pay them. We charge you nothing on top for it, and we confirm it in writing before you sign. See fee-related information

Before you arrive

Relocation plan and timeline; coordination of visa and residence-permit logistics with your immigration lawyer; introduction to a French-American tax advisor; neighborhood guidance and tours; school pre-selection and application calendar; moving budget and checklist.

Your home

Temporary housing coordination; an introduction to a licensed real estate agent, who handles the rental search or purchase, viewings, negotiation and lease; your rental file prepared with you; your calendar organized around the viewings your agent sets; utilities, internet and home insurance setup support; coordination with your movers, who handle customs formalities. Renovation works coordinated under a separate agreement; design by a designer you choose.

Arrival and paperwork

Airport welcome and first-day orientation; bank account opening support; French mobile line; residence-permit logistics and appointments; social security registration; complementary health insurance setup support; driver's license exchange formalities, where the authorities accept your license; car purchase and registration; informal, non-sworn translation of everyday letters; letters with legal, tax or immigration content go to the relevant licensed professional.

Legal, tax and financial, with licensed professionals

Introductions to immigration lawyers; notaries; accountants and tax advisors; banks, including private banking, and wealth managers, who make their own decisions; insurance brokers; sworn translators. These professionals advise you directly, and you pay them directly. We receive no commission from lawyers, notaries, accountants or tax advisors. For any other service, where a commission or referral fee applies, we confirm it in writing before you sign with that provider.

Children and education

Search for international, bilingual, private or public French schools; applications, visits and interviews; public school enrollment at the town hall; daycare and nursery applications; introductions to English-speaking nannies and au pairs; employer paperwork support, quoted separately; French lessons, tutoring and activities; holiday camps.

Health and well-being

Introductions to English-speaking family doctors, pediatricians, dentists and specialists; emergency numbers to keep at hand; gyms, yoga, spas and wellness addresses.

Settling in as a family

Introductions to English-speaking clubs and communities; support for your partner: networking, CV for the French job market, social life.

Daily life and concierge

Home help and housekeeping; nannies and babysitters; groceries; private chef and catering; car rental, drivers and airport transfers; flight, hotel, restaurant and event bookings; tradespeople; pet travel, veterinarian, dog walking and pet sitting; gifts and special occasions; French lessons for adults. Need something else in Paris? Just ask. We confirm in writing what we can take on, and on what terms. Requests that are illegal or unethical are declined.

When you leave Paris

End of lease and move-out inspection; closing contracts and accounts; departure move.
