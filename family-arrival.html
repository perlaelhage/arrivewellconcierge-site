Family Arrival

Cofounded by two mothers, one raising a Franco-American family in Paris.

Book your discovery call

Who it's for

American and international families with children moving to Paris or the Île-de-France region, paying for their own move. If your company pays or reimburses any part of your move, Corporate Arrival applies, on a written quote.

What it brings

One contact who knows your family, and a team behind the scenes. Your rental file, prepared for French landlords when your income comes from abroad. School applications, started early, because schools decide early. The bank, the pediatrician, the family doctor. We keep each one moving, in its turn, so your time can stay with your children, and with Paris.

The founders

Perla is raising a Franco-American family in Paris and has lived in France since 2004. She has 10 years of sales and team leadership at multinational companies, and real estate and interior design experience through her own companies. She is also an interior designer. Christelle has 15 years of experience at prestigious American companies.

What's included

  • A written family plan and timeline, kept up to date
  • Neighborhood guidance and a shortlist of five schools
  • School applications, visits and enrollment paperwork
  • Coordination of residence-permit logistics: appointments, calendar and documents from the list your immigration lawyer provides
  • Housing coordination: an introduction to a licensed real estate agent, who handles the search, viewings, negotiation and lease; a rental file prepared with you for French landlords; your calendar organized around the viewings your agent sets; and a final practical check of the home
  • Coordination of temporary housing that you book and pay for directly
  • Bank, phone, utilities, social security and health insurance setup support
  • Pediatrician, family doctor and dentist introductions, and the emergency numbers to keep at hand
  • Up to 8 in-person appointments
  • A WhatsApp line during our working hours
  • 3 months of follow-up after arrival

Your professionals, coordinated by us

We introduce you to licensed professionals in each field, and you choose who to engage. They advise you directly and invoice you directly. Then, with your agreement, we handle the administrative side with them for the length of your assignment, as set out in your written proposal: the documents they request gathered, appointments booked, the dates they set kept in view.

  • An immigration lawyer, for immigration advice and filings
  • A French-American tax advisor or accountant, for tax advice and filings
  • A licensed real estate agent, for the property search, negotiation and lease. Their fee, typically 1 to 2 months of rent, depends on the contract and the home you choose and is paid directly by you. For some services, such as the home search, we receive a commission from the agent or partner, paid out of the fee you pay them. We charge you nothing on top for it, and we confirm it in writing before you sign.
  • Doctors and hospitals, for medical care. We make the introductions; your health stays between you and your doctor.
  • International movers, sworn translators and notaries
  • Interior designers: if you wish, we introduce you to several. Before any introduction, we tell you in writing when a designer is linked to our company or to one of our cofounders, and the choice is yours. Design and renovation are quoted and invoiced by the professionals you choose. On request, we coordinate works under a separate written agreement.
  • A nanny or household staff: we help you find them, on a separate quote
  • Rent, deposits, school fees, government and visa fees, and movers are also paid directly by you.

How it works

  1. Before arrival, from about 3 months out: your plan, schools, documents and rental file.
  2. Around arrival: move-in, bank, health coverage and first appointments, as each step allows.
  3. After arrival, for 3 months: follow-up on open items, and we close the file with you.
