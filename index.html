How it works

  1. Discovery call. 15 minutes with a cofounder. You tell us your dates and your household. We tell you whether we think we can help, which offer fits and its starting price.
  2. Written proposal. The scope, the price and the terms, in writing, before you decide. If you want a plan first, start with The Arrival Blueprint.
  3. Your arrival. One named contact, and a written record of who does what, by when.
  4. After arrival. Follow-up on open items, then we close the file with you.

We promise the work, in writing.

One named contact. A written record of who does what, by when. A copy of every document we handle for you. A reply by the next working day. If a step is blocked, we tell you what happened and what your options are.

We do not promise a visa, a lease, a school place or a bank account. Those decisions belong to landlords, schools, banks and the authorities.

Discretion is our rule. We never name a client without their agreement, and no professional joins your file without yours.
