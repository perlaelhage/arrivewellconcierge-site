One point of contact

You keep one contact while lawyers, schools, banks and landlords each do their part. We coordinate the steps set out in your written proposal. Licensed professionals, chosen and paid by you, handle legal, immigration, tax and real estate matters.

Introduced, then followed through

We introduce you to licensed professionals: lawyers, tax advisors, real estate agents and English-speaking doctors, as confirmed in your written proposal. You choose who to engage. Then, with your agreement, we handle the administrative side with your lawyer, tax advisor and agent: the documents they request gathered, appointments booked, the dates they set kept in view. The advice is theirs. The decisions are yours. The follow-through is ours.

Read our story

We promise the work, in writing.

We do not promise a visa, a lease, a school place or a bank account. Those decisions belong to landlords, schools, banks and the authorities. Legal, tax and medical advice comes directly from the licensed professional.

You get a copy of every document we handle and a reply by the next working day. If a step is blocked, we tell you what happened and the practical options, and we refer any legal question to the relevant licensed professional.

Discretion is our rule. We never name a client without their agreement, and no professional joins your file without yours. Our privacy policy explains how we handle the information you share with us, and your written proposal sets out the terms for your file.
