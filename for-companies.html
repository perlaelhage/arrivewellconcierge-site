For companies · Corporate Arrival

One contact for HR. One update each week.

Corporate Arrival is for HR and global mobility teams relocating employees and their families to Paris, one assignment at a time or several in a year. If a company pays for the move, Corporate Arrival applies. Your written quote confirms the scope.

What it brings to HR

Your employee has someone beside them for housing, paperwork and daily life, and their family too when your quote includes it. Your HR team has one contact and a clear weekly status. Our work is to give the assignment a solid start.

Christelle has 15 years of experience at prestigious American companies. Perla has 10 years of sales and team leadership at multinational companies. One is your named contact; the other covers absences. Meet the founders

What's included, per employee

  • One contact for HR and one for the employee
  • A written plan and timeline, shared with HR within the limits the employee agrees to
  • Coordination of residence-permit logistics with your immigration counsel
  • Temporary housing support and preparation of the rental file with the employee
  • Bank, phone, utilities, social security and health insurance setup support
  • Up to 4 in-person appointments
  • A weekly status update to HR
  • An end-of-assignment summary of completed and open items
  • 1 month of follow-up after arrival

On request: school support and family follow-up for employees moving with children.

Alongside your advisors

Not part of our quote. We work alongside the advisors your company already has, and introduce others where you need them. With the employee's agreement, we handle the administrative side of each file with them, and report to HR within the limits the employee sets. Their advice remains theirs, and each is engaged and paid directly, never through us.

  • Immigration advice and filings: your immigration counsel, or a licensed immigration lawyer you choose
  • Tax and payroll advice: your advisors, or a licensed tax advisor you choose
  • Home search and viewings: a licensed real estate agent, under terms set out in the proposal
  • International shipping: your moving provider
  • Medical care: doctors. We make the introduction only.

How it works

  1. A first call. To understand your needs and volumes.
  2. A written quote. Per employee, with a named contact.
  3. Kickoff. With the employee, from about 2 months before arrival.
  4. Weekly updates. To HR, until the end of the assignment.

Price: on quote

Quote only, based on the number of employees and the services selected.

  • Quote and scope confirmed in writing before work starts. Payment in full at contract signature.
  • Requests must reach us at least 2 months before arrival.
  • In-person appointments take place on weekdays during the day. During each assignment, we provide in-person and remote support to your employees every month of the year.
  • Paris and Île-de-France. Travel elsewhere is reviewed case by case, on written terms.
  • When the home search involves a licensed real estate agent, the agent may pass part of their fee to us. We add nothing to that fee. Any commission or referral fee is confirmed in writing to your company before the service is confirmed, and your quote states who pays the agent's fee.
  • Cancellation terms are confirmed in writing before you sign.

Our written commitment

  • One named contact for HR, with the second founder covering absences.
  • One written update each week.
  • A reply by the next working day.
  • If a step is blocked, we tell you what happened and what the options are.
  • Medical information is never shared with HR. Other personal data is shared only as the employee agrees in advance.

We do not promise a visa, a lease or a bank account. Those decisions belong to landlords, banks and the authorities. We promise the work.
