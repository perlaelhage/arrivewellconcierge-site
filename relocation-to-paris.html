Relocation to Paris

A relocation to Paris touches your visa, your home, your bank, your health coverage and your daily life, all on different timelines. We give you one contact and one written plan.

Book your discovery call

What a relocation to Paris usually involves

  1. Before you leave: a plan and budget, the right professionals for visa and tax questions, and your documents.
  2. Your home: temporary housing, then a rental file prepared for French landlords, with a licensed real estate agent handling the search and the lease.
  3. Your first weeks: bank, phone, utilities, social security and health insurance.
  4. Daily life: English-speaking doctors, household help and the paperwork that keeps coming.

What we do

We coordinate, prepare and follow up: your plan, your calendar, your documents and your appointments. You get a written record of who does what, by when, and a reply by the next working day.

What licensed professionals do

Immigration lawyers, tax advisors, notaries and real estate agents advise you and act in their own field. You choose them and pay them directly. When a real estate agent handles your home search, the agent pays us part of the fee you pay them. We charge you nothing on top for it, and we confirm it in writing before you sign.

Which offer fits

The Arrival Blueprint if you want a plan first. Family Arrival with children. Executive Arrival without. Corporate Arrival for HR teams. Stay Well for regular concierge support.

Questions about relocating to Paris

How much does a relocation service in Paris cost with you?

Prices: The Arrival Blueprint, €2,250 including VAT (€1,875 excluding VAT). Executive Arrival from €12,000 including VAT (€10,000 excluding VAT) and Family Arrival from €22,500 including VAT (€18,750 excluding VAT). Signature Arrival, €36,000 including VAT (€30,000 excluding VAT). Stay Well, €1,250 per month including VAT, with a 3-month minimum. A real estate agent's fee, typically 1 to 2 months of rent, depends on the contract and the home you choose and comes on top. For some services, such as the home search, we receive a commission from the agent or partner, paid out of the fee you pay them. We charge you nothing on top for it, and we confirm it in writing before you sign.

How early should I contact you?

At least 2 months before you arrive. Families should contact us earlier, because school applications often open months ahead.

Do you handle my visa?

We coordinate the logistics: appointments, calendar and documents from the list your immigration lawyer provides. Advice about your visa comes from the lawyer. We do not promise a visa: that decision belongs to the authorities.

Do you find my apartment?

A licensed real estate agent handles the search, viewings, negotiation and lease. We prepare your rental file with you and organize your calendar. For some services, such as the home search, we receive a commission from the agent or partner, paid out of the fee you pay them. We charge you nothing on top for it, and we confirm it in writing before you sign. We do not promise a lease.

Which languages do you work in?

Perla works in English, French and Arabic. Christelle works in English and French.

Where do you work?

Paris and Île-de-France. Travel elsewhere is reviewed case by case, on written terms.

What happens during the discovery call?

In 15 minutes, you tell us who is moving and when. We tell you whether we think we can help, which offer fits and its starting price. There is no obligation.
